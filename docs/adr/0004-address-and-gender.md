# 0004. Address and gender-inclusive language

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-10-29
- Deciders: Jonas Höchst

## Context

German texts need a decision on how to address readers (du/Sie) and on gender-inclusive forms. Inconsistency here is visible on every page.

## Decision

- Register: neutral and academic by default; most texts do not address the reader.
- Direct address: German **Sie**, English "you".
- Gender: neutral wording where it is common ("Forschende", "Fachleute"); otherwise the gender star ("Kund\*innen"). English uses neutral forms and singular "they".

## Consequences

- Calls to action and forms use Sie ("Schreiben Sie uns").
- The glossary lists the neutral form for person terms where one exists.
