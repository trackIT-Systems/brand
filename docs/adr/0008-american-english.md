# 0008. English texts use American English

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-11-02
- Deciders: Jonas Höchst

## Context

The English website pages and newsletters mix British and American spelling, sometimes within one issue ("rescue centres", "utilise" next to "analyzing", "behavior"). Readers notice this, and a spell checker or Vale style needs one variant.

## Decision

All English texts use American English: spelling (color, analyze, center, behavior), vocabulary and punctuation (double quotes, serial comma).

## Consequences

- Existing English texts on the website and in the newsletter get corrected when they are next edited.
- Vale and spell checkers are configured for en-US.
- Quotes from partners keep their original spelling.
