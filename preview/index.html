Contrast

Approved text and background pairs, taken from the color roles: text, heading and link on page-background and surface, and each on-X on its X. Use these; other pairs are not approved, even when their contrast passes.

All combinations of the palette (for checking, not for use)

WCAG 2.1 contrast of every text color (rows) on every background (columns), computed from the tokens. AA needs 4.5:1 for body text and 3:1 for large text. Framed cells with a dark label are approved pairs.

Logos

Every logo and mark file on each background it could land on. Allowed combinations come from the “Use on” column in assets/README.md; the others are dimmed.
