> The curlew is one of the most endangered meadow-nesting birds in Central Europe. Its population has been declining dramatically for years, primarily because too few chicks survive to adulthood.

> – 35% of the chicks were preyed upon by carnivorous mammals (such as foxes).
> – 18% fell victim to birds of prey.
> – Another chick died in a drainage ditch.
> – Only 12% of the tagged chicks survived until they fledged (depending on the study area and year, the probability of survival ranged from just 0% to 27%).
